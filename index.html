a letter for Mouzia

25.10.2006

where time gave the world a soul,
and life began writing a story
no one had ever read.

a little universe, a story still unfolding.

scroll gently

25 october, 2026

for Mouzia, with love

opening the pages…

a universe made of little things

Some good things arrive quietly.

Touch a point of light and let it join the constellation.

and so it continues

the story is still
yours to write.

happy birthday, Mouzia.
